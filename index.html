Why the Sangweni family started this company

The idea began after a robbery at the student residence where the family’s daughter stayed. One girl was murdered and another was badly hurt, in a place that was not properly secured.

That experience, and a crime wave that leaves many South Africans feeling exposed, drives everything we do. Established in 2025, we aim to deliver affordable, professional security without cutting corners.
